How it flows

01

Escrow the total

Send BOT to the FlowPay contract with a recipient and duration. The full amount is locked upfront.

02

Stream by the second

From the moment your tx is mined, BOT accrues to the recipient linearly at rate = total / duration.

03

Withdraw or cancel, any time

The recipient can withdraw what has streamed so far. The sender can cancel and reclaim the unstreamed remainder.
